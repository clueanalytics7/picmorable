## 🏛️ The Three Foundational Principles

Picmorable is built on three uncompromising principles designed to restore emotional ownership and absolute privacy to personal photography:

1. **Local-First Processing:** Photographs are organised, indexed, deduplicated, and analysed directly on your device. Thumbnails, aspect ratio balancing, EXIF metadata extraction, search indexes, and layout calculations run entirely inside your browser sandbox.
2. **Privacy by Design:** No compulsory cloud uploads, no remote AI processing, no advertising-based data monetisation, and zero tracking pixels. Your private family photographs never leave your machine.
3. **Simple, Intelligent Organisation:** Automatically group photographs by date, cluster event-based collections into a Life Timeline, identify duplicate burst shots, and bind them into a tactile, traditional hardcover memory book.

---

## ✨ Why Picmorable?

Conventional photo applications (such as cloud storage dashboards) often focus on monthly subscription models, remote ingestion, and cloud backup. For someone with 15,000 personal photographs stored across phones, old hard drives, and camera cards, the thought of uploading their life archive to a third party is both intimidating and intrusive.

Physical photo albums hold an enduring emotional and tactile value that generic digital gallery grids don't replicate. Picmorable bridges this gap: it combines the convenience of intelligent, local-first computing with the timeless luxury of a physical, foil-stamped hardcover photo album.

## 🛡️ The Picmorable In-App Privacy Centre

Privacy in Picmorable is not a buried legal disclaimer; it is a living, visible part of the application interface. The **Picmorable Privacy Centre** provides transparent device inspection:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                  Picmorable Privacy Centre                  │
│                     [ Protected ]                           │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  • Photo Processing:        On your device (Client-Side)    │
│  • Cloud Photo Uploads:     Disabled (0 KB transmitted)     │
│  • AI & Arrangement Engine: Local only (Heuristics & APIs)  │
│  • External Photo Sharing:  Not enabled (Offline Sandbox)   │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Live Device Sandbox Monitor:** Displays exact count of photos held in session RAM, buffer memory footprint, and verifies that outbound network requests remain strictly **0**.
- **Verified Metadata vs. User Captions:** Clearly distinguishes verified hardware camera metadata (EXIF timestamps, camera body, lens, orientation) from user-written captions or suggested descriptions. Users retain complete freedom to correct or rewrite any memory note.
- **Immediate Local Data Purge:** One-click sandbox wipe immediately revokes all memory buffers (`URL.revokeObjectURL`), resets album spreads, and flushes RAM. No deletion tickets, no waiting periods.

## 🔍 Duplicate & Burst Detection Engine

- **Intelligent Local Scanning:** Identifies candidate duplicate photos and rapid-fire camera burst sequences (taken seconds apart with identical framing).
- **Side-by-Side Comparison:** Inspect resolution, capture timestamp, and visual similarity side-by-side to choose the definitive **Hero Shot**.
- **Inviolable Deletion Guarantee:** Picmorable **never** automatically deletes original photo files from your computer or phone. Excluded duplicate frames are simply kept off the printed album page spreads.

## 🚀 How to Use Picmorable

1. **Try with 20 Photos:** Click **"Try 20 Photos"** in the top navigation to load our 20-photo sample pack or drop 20 of your own photos.
2. **Review Duplicates:** Click **"Duplicates"** in the top bar to review burst shots and confirm your hero frames.
3. **Explore Life Timeline:** Click **"Timeline"** to see your photos grouped into life event chapters.
4. **Customize Your Hardcover Book:** Flip pages using the arrows or arrow keys (`←`, `→`). Click captions to edit notes. Choose cover cloth and metallic foil.
5. **Download Your Album:** Click **"Download"** to get your Archival PDF or Standalone Offline HTML album.
